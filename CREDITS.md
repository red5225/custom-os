# Credits

CustomOS kernel, terminal, shell foundation, framebuffer UI, and PS/2 mouse support are project code.

## GNU GRUB
CustomOS uses GNU GRUB/Multiboot2 as its current bootloader interface. GRUB is free software under the GNU GPL and related licenses. See the GRUB project for its full license and notices.

## Linux-derived work
Linux-derived drivers are kept separate under third_party/linux and are not the CustomOS kernel. Any future adapted driver must retain upstream copyright and license notices and identify its source/version.

Planned hardware adaptations include USB, Intel AX201 Wi-Fi, storage, and networking.
