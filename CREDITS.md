# Credits

CustomOS kernel, terminal, shell foundation, framebuffer UI, and PS/2 mouse support are project code.

## GNU GRUB
CustomOS currently uses GNU GRUB/Multiboot2 as its bootloader interface. GRUB is free software under the GNU GPL and related licenses.

## Linux-derived work
Linux-derived hardware support is kept separate under third_party/linux. The Linux kernel is NOT the CustomOS kernel. Future adapted drivers must retain upstream copyright and license notices and identify their source/version.
